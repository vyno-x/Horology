Our Horological Mission

Preserving the Art of Mechanical Time

In an era of disposable digital screens, mechanical horology represents lasting human ingenuity, meticulous metallurgy, and timeless craftsmanship.

🔍

100% Verified Authenticity

Every watch undergoes microscopic inspection of the dial printing, case stampings, movement serial numbers, and reference engravings by certified watchmakers.

⏱️

Timing Telemetry & Testing

We test all timepieces across multiple positions using precision electronic timing equipment to evaluate rate deviation, balance amplitude, and beat error.

📜

Complete Provenance Records

We trace historical ownership, manufacturer service invoices, and original papers so that collectors have full confidence in the lineage of their investment.

🛡️

Collector First Guarantee

Every timepiece is delivered with fully insured transport, a multi-point inspection certificate, and an unconditional 14-day return privilege.

The Inspection Protocol

Before any watch is cleared for our Curated Vault, it passes through three stages of validation:

  1. External Condition Audit: Case integrity, lug bevels, bezel detents, sapphire crystal reflections, and original factory finishes.
  2. Calibre Diagnostics: Opening the caseback to verify train wheels, escapement jewels, hairspring concentricity, and winding efficiency.
  3. Pressure & Water Resistance: Vacuum chamber and wet pressure testing to certify depth ratings according to ISO standards.
