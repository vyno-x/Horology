The Art of Mechanical Horology

Physical gear trains regulated by oscillating hairsprings. No batteries. No planned obsolescence.

Enduring Human Craftsmanship

Unlike consumer electronic devices that degrade rapidly, a finely regulated mechanical movement is engineered to operate for decades. Each calibre relies solely on potential energy stored inside a coiled mainspring, released precisely through an escapement beating up to 28,800 vibrations per hour.

Our collection brings together three world-renowned manufactures: Rolex with the archetype of the tool watch; Omega with lunar space exploration; and Tudor with accessible high-performance engineering.
