About Horology

The art and precision of mechanical timekeeping.

Mechanical watches are powered by a wound spring instead of a battery. They represent timeless engineering and human craftsmanship that can last for generations.

Each timepiece in our collection is crafted with high-grade materials, precision-calibrated gears, and durable water-resistant cases.
